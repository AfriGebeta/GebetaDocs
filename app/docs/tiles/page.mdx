# Map Tiles

Gebeta map tiles are detailed vector maps with strong local coverage, ready to drop into a web or mobile app. The SDKs handle the map itself and add what most apps need on top: markers, clustering, routes and shapes, all styled to fit your design.

## Features

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<GlobeIcon />} title="Local detail">
    Rich local data, down to streets, places and points of interest.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<BlendingModeIcon />} title="Styles">
    Standard, satellite and terrain styles, or customize the look to match your brand.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<DrawingPinIcon />} title="Markers">
    Interactive markers with your own images and click handlers.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<GroupIcon />} title="Clustering">
    Group nearby markers automatically so busy maps stay readable.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Pencil1Icon />} title="Shapes">
    Draw routes as lines, and areas as polygons or circles.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<LightningBoltIcon />} title="Fast">
    Tile delivery is optimized for smooth panning and zooming.
  </Card>
</Cards>
